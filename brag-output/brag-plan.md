# Koott — 35s Launch / Explainer Brag Video — Storyboard

## Angle
Koott is the **opposite of an education agent**. It explains itself best as: *type a course in your own words → see whether Australia is actually hiring for it (real government shortage data + salary + your 485 visa) → then meet your people.* The video literally **shows that flow happening** by recreating the real homepage search-box → match-result card in HTML, then names the three real tools and lands on the free / no-agents promise.

## One-sentence product truth (verified from repo)
Koott helps Kerala & Indian students study in Australia by matching a free-text course query to in-demand Australian occupations using real government shortage data — with salaries, the 485 post-study work visa, and a student community. It is free, no agents, no commission.

## Who it's for
Kerala & Indian students planning to study in Australia (the brand's own line: "Built by students who've done it").

## Core flow shown
Search box ("Find the course Australia is actually hiring for.") → progress → match card for **Registered Nurse** (a real entry in the repo's OCC/DEMAND data): In shortage · 13,929 places/yr national cap · median A$76K · 485 post-study stay · real course + visa tags.

## Real features named (all from the live site)
1. Course & Career Advisor — "Tell us what you want to study, in your own words. We match you against the shortage list with real salary ranges and 485 visa pathways."
2. City & University Communities — "Focused chat rooms per city, university and intake. Find your cohort before you land."
3. Visa Compliance Tools — "Track your fortnightly work limit, compare living costs across eight cities, and follow your timeline from CoE to landing day."
