Study in Australia from Kerala: The Complete 2026 Guide

By Koott8 min readUpdated July 2026
Quick answer

To study in Australia from Kerala you need: a course choice (ideally in a field Australia is hiring for), IELTS 6.0–6.5 (or PTE), proof of about A$29,710/year in living funds plus tuition (~A$31,000–51,000/year — regional universities are the affordable end), an offer letter → CoE → Student visa (subclass 500) with OSHC. Start 12–18 months before your February or July intake. Everything below is free to use — koott has no agents and takes no commission.

Every year, students from Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, Thrissur and every town in between board flights to Sydney, Melbourne and — increasingly — regional Australia. Some planned it well and land running. Others paid an agent lakhs for a course that leads nowhere. This guide is the playbook we wish every Kerala student had: the real costs, the real requirements, the timeline, and the one strategic choice (regional) that most agents never mention.

Why Australia works for Kerala students

Three practical reasons, beyond the brochure stuff:

  • Work rights while studying. Student-visa holders can work a capped number of hours (commonly summarised as 48 hours per fortnight) — enough to meaningfully offset living costs, something the US makes far harder.
  • Post-study work by right. The 485 Temporary Graduate visa gives eligible graduates years of full work rights after the degree — and regional graduates can get 1–2 extra years.
  • A skills-shortage economy. Australia publishes exactly which occupations it needs — nursing, aged care, software, cyber security, engineering, teaching and more. Pick a course that feeds a shortage and your job prospects (and visa options) compound. That is the whole idea behind our free Course Advisor.

One honest note on positioning: don't choose Australia to "get PR somehow." Choose a course Australia genuinely needs — the opportunities follow from that, not the other way around.

IELTS & entry requirements

Most universities ask for IELTS 6.0–6.5 overall. As a rule of thumb from the universities in our advisor data: regional universities like Newcastle, Wollongong, UniSA, Flinders, UTAS, JCU, CDU, CQU and UNE accept 6.0, while the University of Adelaide asks 6.5. PTE Academic is widely accepted as an alternative — many Kerala students find it faster to prepare for.

Academic entry is usually your Plus Two marks (for Bachelor's) or degree marks (for Master's); requirements vary by course, so check the university page for your exact program. No agent needed for any of this — universities accept direct applications.

Step-by-step: Kerala → Australia

  1. Pick the field first (12–18 months out). Not the city, not the university — the field. Use the Course Advisor to see which fields are in genuine shortage with real salary bands.
  2. Shortlist 3–5 universities. Balance fees, city costs and the regional advantage (below). Check entry requirements for your specific course.
  3. Take IELTS or PTE. Book early; leave time for one retake.
  4. Apply directly (free for most unis) and collect offer letters.
  5. Accept an offer, pay the deposit, get your CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment).
  6. Arrange money and OSHC. Show the ~A$29,710 financial requirement + tuition; buy Overseas Student Health Cover for the visa length.
  7. Lodge the Student visa (subclass 500) on the Home Affairs website with your CoE, financials, English score and Genuine Student statement.
  8. Land smart. Bank account, TFN, transport card, SIM — our Arrival Kit is the first-week checklist, and our communities mean you know people before you fly.

The regional advantage most agents skip

Here is the single most useful strategic fact in this guide: Australia rewards students who study outside Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane. Cities that count as "regional" for migration purposes include Adelaide, Newcastle, Wollongong, Hobart, Townsville and Darwin — real cities with real universities:

  • Lower tuition — regional universities cluster at the A$31,000–38,500 end.
  • Lower living costs — rent in Newcastle or Hobart is far below Sydney.
  • An extra 1–2 years of 485 post-study work rights for regional graduates — and the regional second-visa charge is far cheaper than the standard 485 fee.

Working while you study

The work cap (commonly summarised as 48 hours per fortnight during study periods, typically uncapped during scheduled breaks) makes a real dent in living costs. Casual rates in retail, hospitality, aged care and warehousing are meaningful money by Kerala standards — but treat work as a supplement, not the funding plan. Our visa tools include a work-hours tracker so you never breach your condition, and our regional jobs guide covers where demand actually is.

After graduation: the 485 visa

The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) is the bridge from degree to career: years of full-time work rights after graduating. Know two things going in: the fee has risen sharply (about A$5,710 for a primary applicant as of 1 July 2026 — full breakdown here), and regional graduates get extra years at a fraction of the cost. Plan for it from day one — it changes which city you should study in.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to study in Australia from Kerala?

Three buckets: tuition (~A$31,000–51,000/year — regional is the affordable end), living costs (the official financial requirement is ~A$29,710/year), and one-offs (visa fee, OSHC, tests, flights). Confirm current figures on Home Affairs and university sites.

What IELTS score do I need?

Most universities ask 6.0–6.5 overall. Many regional universities accept 6.0; University of Adelaide asks 6.5. PTE Academic is widely accepted as an alternative.

How do I start the process?

Work backwards from your intake (Feb/Jul): pick an in-demand field → shortlist universities → IELTS/PTE → direct applications → CoE → financials + OSHC → Student visa (subclass 500). Start 12–18 months out.

Is regional Australia better for Kerala students?

Often, yes: lower tuition and rent, slightly easier entry, and an extra 1–2 years of 485 post-study work rights at a much lower visa charge. Adelaide, Newcastle, Wollongong and Hobart all count as regional.

Can I work while studying?

Yes — a capped number of hours, commonly summarised as 48 hours per fortnight during study periods, typically uncapped in scheduled breaks. Verify the current cap with Home Affairs.
